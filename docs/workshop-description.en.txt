The Best Wigfrid

A Wigfrid enhancement mod for Don't Starve Together.
Original author: 齐天大泽. Maintained and patched by community members.
QQ group: 657495269
Introduction video (Chinese): https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV17mBQYjEYf

Features

All values below use the default strength settings. Some features can be adjusted in the mod configuration. Features disabled by default are marked below.

Elding Spear Pro refers to 武神长矛, Hopeless Hat to 绝境头盔, and Improved Helm Pro to 武神头盔. EX denotes their 真· upgraded variants.

Basic Changes

1. Wigfrid can eat non-meat food. Health, hunger, and sanity effects are scaled to 50% by default. The multiplier is configurable, and non-meat eating can be disabled.
2. Eating meat converts hunger overflow into Inspiration at a default rate of 100%. The conversion rate is configurable.
3. Added: Salt Boxes can store Jerky and Monster Jerky.

Spears

1. Improved: Upgrade a Battle Spear into an Elding Spear with 1 Volt Goat Horn.
2. Improved: The Elding Spear grants 15% movement speed. Lunges restore durability when they hit valid targets.
3. Improved: A Shadow Atrium can be used to charge an Elding Spear.
4. Added: Upgrade a Charged Elding Spear into an Elding Spear Pro using a charged Spark Ark or a Possessed Shadow Atrium.
5. Added: The Elding Spear Pro grants 25% movement speed. Its normal mode deals 68 base damage and 20 planar damage.
6. Added: While Weaponized Warble is active, the Elding Spear Pro switches to a three-hit mode, dealing 17 base damage and 10 planar damage per hit. Normal mode returns when the song effect ends.
7. Added: The right-click skill changes with the attack mode: Sky Lunge in normal mode and a forward lunge in three-hit mode. Both have a 1-second cooldown. Requires the spear AoE option, which is disabled by default, and the corresponding spear skill.
8. Added: Casting Sky Lunge grants 60% damage reduction and protection from hit interruption for 2 seconds. It also allows quick mounting and dismounting. Sky Lunge and forward lunges restore spear durability when they hit valid targets.
9. Added: Battle Spears can be thrown. Requires all three options: Battle Spear upgrades, Battle Spear throwing, and spear AoE skills. Throwing is unavailable with the default configuration.
10. Improved: Spear skills can be used while mounted. Quick casting is enabled by default: hold the right mouse button to aim and release it to cast.

Shield

1. Improved: Removes the shield cooldown on equip.
2. Improved: A successful parry immediately resets the parry cooldown.
3. Improved: Empowered counterattacks after a successful parry restore shield durability.
4. Improved: Wigfrid's shield attacks force the target to keep attacking her for a short period.

Beefalo Riding

1. Improved: The third riding skill can automatically restore Inspiration up to 100.
2. Improved: The second riding skill uses a default riding-duration multiplier of 2. This is configurable.
3. Added: Mounted attacks against valid targets restore 4 health to the Beefalo.
4. Added: Wigfrid can attack with her equipped weapon while mounted.

Helmets

1. Improved: Battle repair can restore the Commander's Helm and its upgraded variants even when Wigfrid is below full health. The corresponding skill is still required.
2. Improved: Upgrade a Battle Helm into a Commander's Helm with 1 Marble.
3. Added: Upgrade a Commander's Helm into a Hopeless Hat with Dreadstone, then into an Improved Helm Pro with an Enlightened Shard.
4. Added: The Hopeless Hat has 80% damage absorption, 60% waterproofness, 5 base planar defense, and 2,000 durability.
5. Added: The Improved Helm Pro has 85% damage absorption, 90% waterproofness, 5 base planar defense, and 1,000 durability.
6. Added: The Improved Helm Pro provides light and can summon Gestalts on attack. Attacks against valid targets also deal bonus damage equal to 0.5% of the target's missing health. This bonus damage is enabled through the option that allows helmet repair below full health.
7. Added: The Hopeless Hat and Improved Helm Pro provide electrical insulation. They remain when their durability reaches zero, but lose base damage absorption, base planar defense, waterproofness, and insulation until repaired.

Battle Songs

1. Improved: Weaponized Warble reduces weapon durability consumption to 25% by default and changes the Elding Spear Pro's attack mode.
2. Improved: Heartrending Ballad grants protection from hit interruption.
3. Improved: Clear Minded Cadenza summons a light that follows the player.
4. Improved: Bel Canto of Courage grants protection from storms.
5. Improved: Fireproof Falsetto makes attacks wet their targets.
6. Improved: Enlightened Lullaby protects against Gestalt attacks.
7. Improved: Dark Lament protects against Shadow Creature attacks.
8. Improved: Rude Interlude adds a sustained taunt and a defense-reducing effect. It also works on bosses.
9. Improved: Startling Soliloquy reduces the target's movement speed to 30%.
10. Improved: Warrior's Reprise restores 75 health to living players, Beefalo whose domestication has begun, and the singer's followers within range. This effect is disabled in PvP mode.
11. Added: Rhapsody restores 20 durability to the singer's held weapon if it uses finite-use durability. Cooldown: 30 seconds. Disabled by default; enable it in the configuration if desired.
12. Improved: The Battle Song Canister has 12 slots by default, with two UI layouts available or the option to keep the original layout. It stays open while moving.
13. Improved: Songs take effect sooner during the singing animation.

Mod Integration

1. Added: Integration with 永恒新界 (New Constant). Upgrade the Elding Spear Pro into an EX Elding Spear Pro using a Dark Soul, increasing damage and durability. When right-click skills are enabled, Sky Lunge also triggers follow-up attacks.
2. Added: Upgrade the Improved Helm Pro into an EX Improved Helm Pro using a Steel Soul. It has 90% damage absorption, 90% waterproofness, 10 base planar defense, 2,000 durability, and 15% movement speed. Bonus damage increases to 1% of the target's missing health.
